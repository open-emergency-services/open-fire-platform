# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

We are firefighters, developers, and public servants building something for our
communities. Treat everyone with the respect you'd want on the fireground: be
welcoming, assume good faith, disagree without contempt, and keep the mission —
free, safe, department-owned software — ahead of ego.

Harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind is not tolerated. Report
concerns to the maintainers at [conduct@your-domain]. Maintainers will respond
confidentially and may remove contributors who violate these norms.
